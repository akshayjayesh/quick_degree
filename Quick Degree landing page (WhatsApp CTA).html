Questions we hear most.

I stopped my degree years ago. Can I still complete it?

In many cases, yes. Depending on your previous credits and the university, you may be able to resume through credit transfer rather than starting over. A consultation is the best first step.

Can I study while working full time?

Yes. Our distance and online programmes are designed for working professionals, with self-paced study and flexible exam windows.

Can I study from the UAE or another country?

Yes. Online and distance programmes can be pursued from anywhere, and our Dubai team supports students across the Gulf.

Which universities do you work with?

We work with UGC-recognised, NAAC-accredited universities. Your advisor will share the specific options that match your profile.

Can you help me get my degree attested for a job abroad?

Yes. We handle embassy attestation, GCC equivalency and WES verification.
